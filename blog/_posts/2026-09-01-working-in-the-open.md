I’m building a directory of the Australian open source ecosystem.

This idea came to me last year. I fell in love with it and did nothing. It sat on the back burner while I worked and lived and then incrementally, just through working and living, the back burner got 
hotter and hotter. Things I was seeing and reading started to coalesce and it became clear to me: The time is now! This thing needs to be in the world. 

🔗 [opensourceaustralia.guide](https://opensourceaustralia.guide/) - a directory to all the things open (source) in Australia.

So I’m building it and it’s fuuuuuunnnn. I always start new projects with ‘The Why’. Why am I even making this thing and who’s it for and why should people care. Then I started fleshing out all the 
things that needed to be done, and woah, like with any project, there’s a lot of hats to wear. Call me [ConcernedApe](https://flicstar.com/stardew) because I’m doing the art, writing the music, and coding the game. 
And it’s all pretty fun, but being a writer I care most about the words. And yes, Claude is helping with stuff I can’t do, like pointing me at rocks to look under, and making the visual design meet accessibility 
standards. Sometimes it’s so overwhelming I just nibble at bits around the sides, dipping my toe in here and there and going down a few rabbit holes when I have five minutes to spend.

People have tried this kind of thing before and I can see the link rot, the domain squatting, and their abandoned sites as I 
trundle by. Who’s to say mine will be any different? It doesn’t matter - **build it anyway** is the very clear message I’m getting. Maybe no one needs a centralised infohub of all this stuff but 
you know what? I’m doin’ it anyway.  
   
Come join me over there. I’m building it now - get in touch and tell me what I'm missing, who I should talk to, and what else it could be. Working in the open, babeeee.
