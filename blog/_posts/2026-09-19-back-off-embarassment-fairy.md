When you work remote on your personal project, it’s cosy in your home office with no one to nay-say you. Real Cosy.

🔗 [opensourceaustralia.guide](https://opensourceaustralia.guide/) - there's nothing wrong with it, right?! Chef's kiss, mwah.

But after working in a vacuum for a month, I knew it was time to send some sonar pings and listen for what comes back.

The glorious vision I have of this site being built in the open by the community means that, at some point, I need to tell people it exists 
and invite them to come play. But it’s difficult to cross that threshold. And aren’t we all scaredy cats sometimes? 

I have a tenuous connection with an open source rockstar and when I reached out, she’s so classy she sent me the exact thing I needed to hear 
at that moment: [Embrace the chaos](https://www.webchick.net/embrace-the-chaos).

To become Sloppy Sam I need to get the site out in front of people. I have emailed the Linux Australia mailing list, shared on my socials, 
and submitted to Everything Open 2027\. And now we wait…
